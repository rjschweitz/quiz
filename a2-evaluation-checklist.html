Where the sample differs from the template

  • The template's UX journal table has a "Major factor affecting the UX" column. The sample swapped it for "Quality of user experiences". Including both is the safe option.
  • The sample is incomplete. It has no Conclusion, and its Limitations section is only a heading, so those parts come from the template alone.
  • The sample's scenarios section points to the wrong appendix. Check every appendix reference before you submit.
